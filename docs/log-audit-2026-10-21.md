# October 21, 2026 log categorization audit

The audit uses a fixed snapshot of 70,976 captured observations from October 18
through October 21 at 04:49:03 EDT (08:49:03 UTC). The installed app continued
writing logs, so the before and after runs use the same saved input. The session
archive adds no independent evidence: its 23,952 unique timestamp/text pairs all
overlap this snapshot; 271 repeated archive rows are excluded.

The recordings contain 57 historical unknown entries. The released parser already
recognizes 30 of those. This patch recognizes another 12, leaving 15 unknown
observations across 11 distinct texts:

| Newly recognized pattern | Observations | Handling |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Kindness faction reaction | 4 | Considered NPC target, without combat credit. |
| Guild message header | 3 | Personal notice; authored combat or XP text stays inside the notice. |
| Named NPC's unnamed pet | 1 | The pet is the separate damage source; the printed 5 damage is preserved. |

The same replay also corrects fields on messages that were already categorized:

Existing numbered damage and healing values, looted coin and split payouts remain
unchanged. The newly recognized pet hit contributes its explicit 5 damage; the
manual redistribution does not attach a payout to an earlier corpse loot.
Twenty-five historical Dummy Fix estimates remain unreadable attempts when
reparsed: the raw text does not contain enough information to recover their
damage or healing amounts.

Complete quoted chat and guild notices also retain their authored payload, so an
embedded `You gain experience!` does not become an XP tick. Genuine adjacent
combat, faction and XP messages still split into independent events.

## October 22 consider follow-up

New captures show named NPC faction reactions followed by one or two difficulty
sentences. OCR sometimes drops the visible `--` separator. NPC classification
therefore follows explicit faction wording, including `considers you with
kindness`, rather than capitalization or name rarity. Bare player difficulty
messages, including `<player> They look weak.`, must not open the NPC card.
All 44 distinct historical consider messages remain recognized. A complete
consider with two difficulty clauses also preserves a following numeric hit,
including when the literal separator is present.

## Remaining evidence

The 15 remaining observations contain five ambiguous lowercase-name combat/cast
lines, three fragments of chat, two bare `Damage.` tails, two diet/UI lines, two
narrative emotes and one bare name. No earlier unambiguous capital-I spelling
establishes the lowercase-name correction. These observations remain unknown
instead of introducing guessed names, damage or Utility.

Regression tests use fictional names and compact structural fixtures. Private
chat, full personal logs and the local archive are not committed.

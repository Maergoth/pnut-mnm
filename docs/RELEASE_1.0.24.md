# PNUT M&M 1.0.24

- Hover settings gears float over each overlay's existing content. They do not reserve a column, add padding or enlarge a compact timer/attack bar. Short bars use a smaller gear; hover visibility, click-through and settings shortcuts still apply.
- NPC respawn timers created through the right-click menu show the same monster icon used on the map, centered inside the radial countdown. The icon follows the timer's normal, warning and expired colors, remains visible after expiration and stays when that timer is restarted. Other timers retain their existing display.
- Overlay gears open the main app at the relevant options and briefly outline the destination; the timer gear highlights its timer list in Triggers. Reduced motion uses a steady brief outline.
- Weapon delay is visible beside the auto-attack toggle in Settings > Overlay. Enter weapon-stat units (36 = 3.6 seconds), or 0 for automatic timing. This immediate preference stays synchronized with the bar's right-click editor and preference backups, without clearing learned swings.
- The Live page and tray now say **End Encounter** for the action that closes the current fight and retains its results.
- Mini stats retain current and maximum weight. Two fresh over-capacity readings start a gray screen-edge pulse, which stops after a valid reading at or below capacity. The **Overburdened warning** option in **Mini stat window** disables it independently of low-health alerts. Red health warnings take priority when both are active; reduced motion keeps the edge glow steady.
- Hover the combat-chat or mini-stat screen read area to reveal its top-left settings gear. It follows the captured game window and opens the relevant highlighted options. The rest of the read area continues passing input to the game.
- The main **Ranks** page opens an anonymous **Leaderboard** with personal lifetime kills, deaths, highest hit and coin, plus maximum HP/Mana. **Copy** stays local; separate **Submit** publishes at most once per UTC day and requires fresh **Window Capture** mini stats. Desktop capture can copy fresh stats but cannot submit or add leaderboard totals. Cached public entries remain visible offline; use **Refresh public entries** to check for updates. An optional secret phrase restores the same anonymous three-word identity. Copy and Submit are also separate mini-stat hover shortcuts.
- The notable NPC menu puts levels before names. A sparkled sword marks known unique loot; a feather marks NPCs offering quests or required by quests. Cached details appear immediately and missing details load in the background.
- A fresh notable-NPC kill by you, your party or an owned pet plays a short synthesized victory fanfare and opens the NPC card, using only the current captured zone's notable list. The sound has its own toggle under Considered NPC and follows master volume/mute; the card respects its existing display settings. Cached lists work offline. Old logs, demos and duplicate slay/death notices do not replay celebrations.
- The main app's **Zones** page browses maps and notable NPCs independently of the live map. A permanent header provides zone, map-floor and NPC dropdowns; the adjacent NPC pane shares field settings, wiki links and lazy cached item tooltips, and works with overlays hidden or disabled. Its saved zone and map view stay separate from the live overlay.
- Cached wiki details appear immediately and viewed records receive periodic revision checks. Changed page or template data refreshes in the background while unchanged content is reused. Caves of Irem now offers its primary map before the hand-drawn alternate, including after refreshing an older alternate-only cache.
- Fresh installations include an enabled **Resist** starter trigger. When a target resists your spell, it speaks **resist** without starting a timer. Existing profiles retain their trigger choices; **Restore starter triggers** can add it explicitly.

Windows regression, startup and extracted portable-package checks gate publication. Installed copies update through **Settings > App updates** after this release is published.

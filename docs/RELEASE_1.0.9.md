# PNUT M&M 1.0.9

- **Mini stat window:** select an optional **Separate stats region** in Settings. Keep the panel clear of chat; its text stays out of chat parsing.
- **Low HP:** two fresh readings below 10% can sound an alert and briefly flash red screen edges. Customize the sound, volume and flash under Mini stat window.
- **Estimated XP/hour:** Session compares stable XP-bar readings about five minutes apart. The yellow bar and thin blue sub-bar supply an estimate; the footer shows a rate after the first complete interval. Missing readings restart the comparison while retaining measured totals.
- **Mez breaks:** expand counts by person to see target and time. A single nearby hit on an observed mesmerized target identifies a likely breaker; unclear breaks remain separate. Carebear Mode keeps your own details visible.
- **Revenge List:** tighter rows, **+** beside the Players and Guilds headings, and click either heading to copy that list's CSV.

Download `PNUT-MnM-1.0.9-windows.zip`, extract it into a writable folder and run `PNUT M&M.exe` with `_internal` beside it. Installed copies can use **Settings > App updates**.

# October 10, 2026 log categorization audit

The audit covers 64,282 captured observations from October 8 through October 10 at
01:07, using a fixed cutoff because the installed app continued writing logs. The
recordings contained 226 unknown entries. The updated parser recognizes 141 of them:

| Category | Previously unknown entries now recognized | Behavior |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Coin | 113 | Mixed denominations and loot splits sum to copper; impossible splits are rejected. |
| Damage | 10 | Extra undead damage from Poisonous Deluge, Surging Shadows and Rot Flesh keeps its actor, skill, target and printed amount. |
| Status/actions | 10 | Dispel Magic and disease recovery retain their respective action/status fields. |
| Consider | 4 | Corpse examination stays separate from kills and loot. |
| Experience | 3 | A single OCR punctuation glyph after the complete experience message is tolerated. |
| Cannot attack | 1 | A known facing failure with a misread punctuation glyph stays a failed action, with no Utility credit. |

The same logs also contained messages that previously parsed into the wrong category or
lost their target:

| Message | Correct handling |
| --- | --- |
| `a dusty skeleton begins to bleed profusely.` | Damage effect; no invented damage amount or Utility point. |
| `a dusty skeleton is bleeding out.` | Damage effect; numbered Bleed ticks still count as damage. |
| `a dusty skeleton is struck by a barbed arrow.` | Damage effect; no extra Utility credit. |
| `You fire an exposing shot at a dusty skeleton.` | Named action on the correct target; defaults to Utility when no damage is associated with it. |
| `a dusty skeleton looks weak.` | Weakness debuff; credit follows identifiable nearby actions, including Omen of Enfeeblement and Exposing Shot. |
| `a dusty skeleton breaks free of the webs.` | Root fade, not another landed effect. |
| `You dispel magic from a dusty skeleton. (Holy Fortitude)` | Named Dispel Magic action; enemy dispels count as Utility. Friendly dispels do not use this combat fallback. |

Replaying the raw logs rebuilt 180 encounters. All 629 Bleed, Barbed Arrow and profuse
bleeding announcements earned zero Bleed Utility and kept their damage amount unset.
Damage remained attached to the numbered hit/tick messages. The new fallback credited
89 Exposing Shot actions and 12 other named non-damage actions; buff prose and melee
verbs did not earn fallback Utility.

## Attribution rules

A player action needs an identifiable actor, target and skill. Its target must be a
confirmed enemy that participated in combat; a harmless NPC greeting or buff sentence
does not establish that. Named enemies and attributed pets use the same encounter identities
as the meters. Cast announcements without a target do not get generic fallback credit.

Known damage actions, unreadable damage attempts, failed/resisted/immune attempts and
effect fades are excluded. An action already credited for a landed CC or debuff effect
does not receive an additional fallback point. Non-damage secondary effects, such as
Distress reducing resistance, can still earn Utility alongside their skill's damage.

Older saved events that classified Bleed or Barbed Arrow as debuffs are corrected on
import, and the aggregation layer also rejects their historical Utility credit.

## Remaining entries

The remaining 85 unknown entries primarily contain system/spell-list text, chat or incomplete
OCR fragments. Bare `Damage.` tails lack an actor, target and amount; they remain unknown
instead of creating guessed damage or Utility. Fused messages with unreadable fields also
remain unknown when the existing line-splitting and name-repair rules cannot recover them.

Armor-break messages are recognized as status. The logs do not reliably identify which
player caused the separate armor effect, so it is not attributed to an arbitrary nearby
damage ability.

Older October 4 development recordings contained 988 historical unknown observations.
Before this update, the current parser already recognized 167 of them. The remainder
is largely older OCR or clipped text, rather than another clearly identifiable new combat
message format. Private chat and full personal logs are not included in this repository.

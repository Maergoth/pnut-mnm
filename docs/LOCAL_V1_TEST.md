# Local 1.0.2 checks

Use `dist/publish/v1.0.2/PNUT M&M/PNUT M&M.exe`, or extract
`releases/PNUT-MnM-1.0.2-windows.zip` into a writable folder and run its executable.
Keep `_internal` beside it. Use a separate folder when testing fresh preferences.

1. Setup offers character/mode, crop/OCR, then **Common options**: **Revenge List**, **Display map**, **Auto attack bar**, and **Copy to clipboard after fight**. Back preserves choices; Cancel leaves current preferences alone. Capture preview and OCR text remain visible in either mode; closing setup clears its image. A successful OCR test enables Continue. Help can reopen setup.
2. Carebear starts on. Check your own performance and eligible group averages on Live, Session, overlays, history, clipboard and file exports. Damage/DPS needs three active contributors with damage at least their healing; Healing/HPS needs three with healing at least their damage. Positive ties count in both; inactive members count in neither. Smaller cohorts show **—** and omit that average from exports. Individual groupmate performance stays hidden. Verify a 25% actual own damage share displays 25% for you and 75% on the group row, independently of its average. Enter a pet name with **Assign a pet to me…** and clear it through **My pets**; assigning to another owner should be unavailable.
3. Click the flower label to open **Morality Adjustment**. Clicking or making a short drag keeps Carebear. Drag most of the way right to request **Elitist Scumbag Mode**. Cancel, Escape and closing the pledge keep Carebear; accepting the promise enables individual performance details. Returning to Carebear removes those details immediately. Check the sad slider emoji, flames, mute and reduced animations.
4. In Carebear, use Feed search/copy, capture preview/OCR and **OCR Diagnosis** normally. Check saved trigger names/patterns, captured matches, regex values, speech, custom sounds, manual timers, previews and JSON/chat sharing. Switching modes should keep these usable, including received sender details and personal rebuff alerts.
5. Enable **Revenge List** in either mode. Check its panel, overlay tab, **Pop out**, arbitrary manual Add/Remove and incoming PvP prompts: right-hand **+**, 30-second expiry, once per attacker per session. Automatic detection needs your character name; manual Add does not. Ignore named NPCs. Verify newest-first ordering and **30 days / 100 entries** defaults; **All** removes each display limit without deleting entries. Changing modes keeps the list and open Add dialog usable. Disabling Revenge hides its panel, tab and pop-out.
6. Check restored map/current-zone labels. A completed known-zone entry or local `/who` summary should correct a stale zone; a `/who` response during combat should keep the fight open. Incomplete, unknown-zone and game-wide summaries should not select a map. Manual selection remains available when no zone evidence is present.
7. Import a log, cancel another import, browse previous sessions and page through older fights. Imported filenames and local logs remain accessible in Carebear; performance views/exports remain filtered. In full mode, edit an archived fight's group/pet attribution and reload it. Resume a prior session only while stopped.
8. Save/apply a named capture profile and create a full preference backup in Carebear. Restore should preserve the current log folder and return to Carebear. Try Help's labeled demo and confirm it cannot export fictional data.
9. Try Stop/Start, unavailable-game recovery, DPI/monitors, keyboard navigation and a longer gameplay session. Record incorrect totals, capture stalls or growing memory use with reproduction steps.

Carebear filters individual performance comparisons. Ordinary captured chat, triggers,
settings, capture/OCR, diagnostics, backups and an enabled Revenge List remain available.
Published packages require a clean committed build and are available through
[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Maergoth/pnut-mnm/releases).

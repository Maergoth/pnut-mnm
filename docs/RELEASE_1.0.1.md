# PNUT M&M 1.0.1

Casual Mode is now **Carebear Mode** throughout the app, setup, overlays and exports. It remains on by default, with the same deliberate slider and teammate promise for Elitist Scumbag Mode.

Group combat averages now compare the same roles:

- **Damage/DPS:** includes active group members whose damage is at least their healing.
- **Healing/HPS:** includes active group members whose healing is at least their damage.
- Pets count with their owners. Positive ties count in both; inactive members count in neither.
- Each role needs at least three active contributors. Smaller cohorts show **—**; CSV and JSON exports leave those values empty. Your own values stay exact, and other group averages are unchanged.

The Damage tab keeps your actual percentage of group damage; the group row shows the remaining percentage. These shares are independent of the displayed averages. Personal rebuff timers keep useful static titles while captured chat and player details remain protected.

Download **PNUT-MnM-1.0.1-windows.zip**, extract it into a writable folder, and keep `_internal` beside **PNUT M&M.exe**. Existing installations can use PNUT's Update option. The companion `.zip.sha256` file records the package checksum.
